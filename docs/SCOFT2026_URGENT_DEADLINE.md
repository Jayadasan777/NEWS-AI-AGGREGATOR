## 🎯 CRITICAL FOCUS AREAS

### 1. AI Detection Risk 🔴 HIGH PRIORITY
**Problem:** If your paper was drafted/revised with Claude Code (which it was), AI detectors may flag sections.

**High-Risk Sections:**
- Abstract (formulaic structure)
- Introduction (perfect transitions)
- Methodology descriptions (too systematic)
- Results interpretation (balanced phrasing)

**Humanization Checklist:**
```
Before: "We present NISE, a clustering algorithm which uses..."
After: "In this work, we introduce NISE, our approach that leverages..."

Before: "The experimental results demonstrate significant improvements..."
After: "Our experiments show meaningful gains..."

Before: "The proposed methodology achieves superior performance..."
After: "NISE achieves strong performance..."
```

**Add Human Voice:**
- "We discovered that..."
- "Surprisingly, our initial approach..."
- "Through extensive experimentation, we found..."
- "One challenge we faced was..."

### 2. Plagiarism Threshold
**Acceptable:** < 10% similarity (excluding references/bibliography)  
**Target:** Aim for < 8% to be safe

**Common Sources of High Similarity:**
- Methodology descriptions (field-standard terms)
- Mathematical definitions (universal notation)
- Related work (citing others' contributions)
- Experimental protocols (standard procedures)

**These are usually EXCLUDED from similarity count, but verify:**
- Reference list
- Author names
- Equations (if standard)
- Block quotes (if properly cited)
