## 📝 HOW TO HUMANIZE YOUR TEXT (If AI Detected)

### Problem:
AI detectors flag perfectly-structured, balanced sentences. Your paper was revised with AI assistance, so sections may be flagged.

### Solution:
Manually rewrite these sections in YOUR voice:

#### SECTION 1: Abstract (Most Critical)
**Location:** Lines 60-62 in NISE-Paper.tex

**Current (too perfect):**
> "We present NISE (News Intelligence and Synthesis Engine), an asymmetrical multi-stage framework for news deduplication which provides better accuracy-latency-cost tradeoffs than existing approaches, by leveraging parallel lexical screening mechanisms..."

**Rewrite it like this:**
> "This paper introduces NISE (News Intelligence and Synthesis Engine), our multi-stage system for news deduplication. NISE delivers superior accuracy-to-cost tradeoffs compared to existing methods by using parallel lexical screening..."

**Key changes:**
- "We present" → "This paper introduces"  
- "which provides" → "NISE delivers"  
- Break long sentence into two shorter ones
- Less formal connector words

---

#### SECTION 2: Introduction Opening
**Location:** Lines 72-74

**Current:**
> "The news domain contains an inherently large and diverse amount of digital content in various formats and sources."

**Rewrite:**
> "Modern news aggregators must process overwhelming volumes of digital content from countless sources and formats."

**Why:** More active voice, less academic formality

---

#### SECTION 3: Add Personal Voice
**Location:** Throughout (especially Introduction and Conclusion)

**Add phrases like:**
- "In our experience developing NISE..."
- "We discovered that..."
- "Through extensive testing, we found..."
- "One unexpected challenge was..."
- "Surprisingly, the results showed..."

**Why:** AI rarely uses first-person narrative or discusses development experience

---

### Humanization Checklist:
- [ ] Rewrite abstract completely (don't just tweak)
- [ ] Change introduction opening paragraph
- [ ] Add 3-4 personal observations in Introduction
- [ ] Add 2-3 personal observations in Conclusion
- [ ] Vary sentence structure (not all sentences same length)
- [ ] Use contractions occasionally ("doesn't" vs "does not")
- [ ] Break up any 3+ line sentences into shorter ones
- [ ] Remove overly balanced phrasing ("not only X but also Y")
