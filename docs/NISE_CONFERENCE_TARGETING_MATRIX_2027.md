# NISE Conference Targeting Matrix (checked 14 October 2026)

## Purpose and caveat

This is a venue-neutral shortlist for the NISE paper on cost-aware news/event clustering, lexical and multi-signal candidate gating, LLM verification, and a deployed news aggregation system. Dates are taken from the official conference pages available on 14 October 2026. Calls can change; re-check the official page before submitting.

**No venue can be treated as a guaranteed acceptance.** The present manuscript is an audit-revised working draft. The benchmark and model-execution evidence must be reconciled before a full empirical research submission is ready. Do not submit the current draft as though its results are validated.

## Track-fit notes

### ECIR 2027 Demo — best near-term system-showcase route
Official CFP: https://www.ecir2027.co.uk/call-for-demos

The call asks for a live online demo or a video showing the main features, encourages public source code, and allows up to four pages plus references. Use the Springer LNCS template. This track evaluates the research prototype/system; it is not a substitute for proving the paper's quantitative clustering claims.

### NAACL 2027 Industry Track — possible but time-sensitive
Official CFP: https://2027.naacl.org/calls/industry_track/

The official call page should be checked directly because details may still be updated. The indexed call lists 31 October 2026 as the submission deadline and emphasizes real-world NLP implementations, operational maturity, and clear/reproducible evaluation. A system deployed in practice may fit, but unsupported production metrics are a serious weakness.

### SIGIR 2027 — likely strongest full research fit
Official CFP: https://sigir2027.org/pages/submit-full.html

The full-paper page lists proposed dates of 14 January 2027 (abstract) and 21 January 2027 (paper). This is a promising target if NISE is positioned as a cost-aware event-matching method with reproducible same-split comparisons, carefully measured call reduction, cost, recall, precision, and latency—not merely as an application description.

### ACL 2027 — conditional NLP fit
Official CFP: https://2027.aclweb.org/calls/main/

The ARR submission deadline is 4 January 2027. ACL is appropriate only if the central contribution is meaningful for NLP researchers and the empirical work is substantial. The submission must use the relevant ACL/ARR format and policies; the IEEE six-page master is not directly reusable as the final submission.

### WSDM 2027 — future cycle
Official site: https://www.wsdm-conference.org/2027/

The main-paper deadline is past. Its stated emphasis on principled web search/data-mining models and in-depth accuracy/performance analysis makes it a possible future target after a stronger benchmark and experimental study.

## Other venues to monitor

- CIKM (ACM International Conference on Information and Knowledge Management): monitor the next official call for information retrieval, knowledge discovery, and applied AI tracks.
- The next SIGIR, ECIR, WSDM, and ACL-family cycles: track their official calls and workshop tracks.
- Relevant workshops colocated with major IR/NLP conferences: consider only when the workshop scope specifically matches event detection, news understanding, LLM evaluation, information integrity, or cost-aware NLP systems.

Do not invent a deadline or assume a call is open when the official CFP is not published.

## Submission integrity rules

1. Do not submit the same paper to multiple concurrent proceedings venues where dual submission is prohibited.
2. A venue-specific version may change framing, length, template, and emphasis, but it must preserve the same factual evidence and results.
3. Keep the production system and the research evaluation distinct: a live demo does not validate a benchmark metric.
4. Verify author consent, authorship order, affiliation, and corresponding-author details with all co-authors before submission.
5. Check the official rules on anonymity, preprints, AI writing assistance, data availability, artifact review, copyright, and page limits for each venue.
6. Confirm the exact current CFP and deadline on the venue's official site on the day of submission.
