# NISE Conference Targeting Matrix (checked 14 October 2026)

This is a venue-screening shortlist, not a guarantee of acceptance, indexing, or eligibility. Official calls can change; re-check them before submitting. Do not submit the same manuscript simultaneously to multiple conferences or review systems when their policies prohibit concurrent submissions.

## Research fit

NISE currently concerns news-event matching and clustering, lexical similarity, evidence fusion, selective LLM verification, evaluation methodology, and operational cost. Venue fit depends on producing a valid, independently labeled benchmark and reproducible comparisons. The present repository audit found unresolved evidence conflicts, so the manuscript is not ready to submit yet.

## Recommended order

1. **First, finish the evidence work.** Freeze one dataset; independently label and adjudicate it; execute the actual code path; compare all baselines on the same pairs; archive per-pair predictions and configuration.
2. **Then target SIGIR 2027** if its official proposed dates are confirmed and the paper reaches the expected empirical standard.
3. **Evaluate ACL 2027 via ARR** if the contribution is genuinely methodological and fits its NLP scope; review the exact ARR rules and commitment dates.
4. **Keep ECNLPIR and applied-systems events as conditional alternatives** only after a quality and publication-venue check.
5. **Do not target every conference indiscriminately.** Choose one venue at a time based on fit and rules; use later cycles for a substantially improved version if the first target is unsuitable.

## Required checks for every candidate

- Official conference site and current call for papers
- Research topics and track fit
- Exact abstract, paper, registration, and camera-ready deadlines
- Publisher and proceedings series
- Indexing confirmed through the index/publisher itself, not only organizer claims
- Peer-review model, acceptance statistics if officially published, and reputation
- Page limit and template
- Anonymity, preprint, AI-use disclosure, authorship, and ethics requirements
- Registration, travel/attendance requirements, publication fees, and student funding
- Prior publication and concurrent-submission rules
