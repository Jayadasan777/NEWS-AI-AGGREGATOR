KEY INSIGHTS
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. CEILING PERFORMANCE
   - LLM achieves near-perfect classification when called for every pair
   - Expected range: 97-100% accuracy
   - This is the theoretical maximum the system can achieve

2. COST-ACCURACY TRADE-OFF
   - Perfect accuracy (100%) costs $11.60 per 1M pairs
   - Production 2-stage saves 82.2% of LLM calls at cost of ~37% accuracy
   - Effective cost reduction: $11.60 → $7.52 (35% cheaper)
   - Accuracy trade-off: 100% → 62.63% (37% reduction)

3. NO FREE LUNCH THEOREM
   - Cannot achieve both perfect accuracy AND high cost savings
   - Must choose optimization target: accuracy or efficiency
   - Current system optimizes for cost/latency over accuracy

4. ROOM FOR IMPROVEMENT
   - Gap between LLM-only (100%) and production (62.63%) = 37.37%
   - Indicates filtering/gating is primary bottleneck
   - Improving recall of EFSA gate could bridge this gap
   - Alternative: Relax thresholds, accept higher LLM call rate

5. STATISTICAL CONFIDENCE
   - With N=198: 95% CI for 100% accuracy is [98.10%, 100.00%]
   - With N=250: 95% CI for 100% accuracy is [98.49%, 100.00%]
   - With N=883: 95% CI for 100% accuracy is [99.57%, 100.00%]
   - Confidence intervals tighten with larger sample sizes
