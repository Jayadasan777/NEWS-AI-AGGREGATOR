STATISTICAL INTERPRETATION
================================================================================

Wilson Score Interval Properties:
- More accurate than normal approximation for small samples
- Asymmetric intervals (not symmetric around point estimate)
- Never extends beyond [0, 1] bounds
- Recommended for proportions with n < 30 or p near 0/1

Key Observations:
1. Non-overlapping CIs indicate significant differences
   - LLM-Only vs all others: CIs do not overlap → significant
   - Production vs traditional baselines: CIs barely overlap → likely significant
   - EFSA+DPCS vs traditional: CIs overlap substantially → may not be significant

2. Wide CIs for precision in low-recall methods reflect uncertainty
   - Jaccard: only 2 positive predictions → very wide precision CI
   - Production: 25 positive predictions → moderate precision CI

3. F1-Score CIs computed via delta method approximation
   - Accounts for covariance between precision and recall
   - May be conservative for very low recall cases
